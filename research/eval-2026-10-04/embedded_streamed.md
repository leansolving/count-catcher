# Kernel-only run and embedded against streamed (2026-10-04)

Data record of two measurements: the Lean kernel alone against native evaluation on a forward
refutation, and embedded against streamed certificates. Raw outputs on the cluster in `/home1/szeider/mccert/eval1004/`
(`m1/` measurement 1, `m2/` measurement 2, `logs/`, `jobs.txt`, the two Slurm scripts).

## Measurement 1: kernel mode against native mode on the domino forward refutation

### Result in short

The earlier observation that the kernel alone does not finish the forward proof of the domino
tilings of a 4x4 board does not hold with lrat-catcher v0.2.0 and the final pipeline. The
kernel finishes the 4x4 refutation in 24 s (keep mask and inlining) to 78 s (untrimmed, the
form of the 2026-09-29 record), and the 4x6 board in 157 s, against 16 to 18 ms natively. It
does not finish the 6x6 board: its memory grows by about 0.3 GB/s and reaches the 64 GiB limit
after about 235 s. The bound is memory, not time; the 3600 s limit was never reached.

### Protocol

- Instances: domino tilings of the 4x4 board (`scope/cnf/dom04.cnf`, identical to
  `gen_domino.py 4` by `cmp`; 24 variables, 68 clauses, 36 tilings), and for the frontier the
  4x6 board (281 tilings) and the 6x6 board (6,728 tilings) from `gen_domino.py 4 6` and `6 6`.
- Certificate with the final tools (`tools_final/bin`): `d4 in.cnf -dDNNF -out=in.nnf`,
  `cpog-gen-fwd -F -T 500 -G in.cnf in.nnf cert.cpog` (4x4: 7,942 bytes), then `cpog2lrat` to
  text LRAT (kernel mode parses the file at elaboration time) in three forms:
  - `full`: `--delete` only (the form of the 2026-09-29 record, 332 clauses, 194 additions),
  - `trim`: `--delete --trim` (the embedded default), forward CNF with all clauses,
  - `ki`: `--delete --keep --inline` (the streamed default without `--binary`), forward CNF
    with only the kept clauses (`ki.cnf`, written by `--keep`).
- Lean files (generated by `eval1004/m1.sbatch`), `import LRATCatcher.Reflect`, lrat-catcher
  v0.2.0 `b2e8d59` as built in `lean_final` (oleans through `LEAN_PATH`, no lake), Lean 4.30.0:
  - kernel: `lrat_reflect +kernel NAME "V.cnf" "V.lrat"`: the CNF embedded as a literal term,
    the LRAT pre-parsed into a `List IntAction` literal, discharged by `decide +kernel`; the
    command sets `maxHeartbeats 0` and `maxRecDepth 1000000` itself; axioms of the theorem:
    `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound` only.
  - native: `lrat_reflect NAME "V.cnf" "V.lrat"` (`native_decide`, both plugins loaded).
  - elaboration only: the kernel file with `set_option debug.skipKernelTC true` (the theorem
    is elaborated but not checked by the kernel), which separates the kernel's share.
- Limits: `timeout -k 30 3600 lean FILE`, Slurm `--mem=64G -c 2 -C genoa`. Wall time and peak
  RSS of the Lean process from getrusage of the children (Python wrapper as in `e2e.sbatch`),
  VmHWM and VmRSS sampled every 5 s (`*.mem`). Command time: difference of the
  `IO.monoMsNow` stamps around the `lrat_reflect` command (elaboration plus kernel check).
- Node: slurm-cpu01 (AMD EPYC 9554P), shared with other users' jobs; one repetition. Jobs
  126711 (4x4 `trim`), 126712 (4x4 `ki`), 126713 (4x4 `full`), 126714 (4x6 `ki`), 126715
  (6x6 `ki`).

## Measurement 2: embedded against streamed

### Result in short

On 11 instances whose text LRAT (2.7 to 132 MB) can be embedded, embedding brings no
measurable gain in the time of the Lean process: that time (4 to 13 s) is dominated by start-up
and imports and varies by up to a factor of 2.5 between repetitions of the same run. The check
time that `count_models` reports repeats within 5 percent on most runs (at most 21 percent,
homer S2) and shows two effects:

- On the same text LRAT, the embedded check is about 10 percent faster than the streamed one
  (embedded/streamed 0.90 in the median, range 0.82 to 0.93).
- Against the streamed default (keep mask, inlining, binary), which an embedded certificate
  cannot use, the embedded check is slower by a factor of 1.86 in the median (1.41 to 2.12).

Memory differs little, since about 3.4 GB of every run is the imports (the warm-up run,
which only imports `McCertTool`, peaks at 3.47 GB): embedded/streamed 1.04 (1.00 to 1.16) on
the same file and 1.06 (1.00 to 1.42) against the streamed default; the largest difference is
cub100 (132 MB embedded LRAT: 5.8 GB against 4.1 GB).

### Notes on the metric

- The convention of the ablation factors is "without the choice over with it". With "embedded
  certificate" as the choice, that is streamed/embedded (the inverse column). The whole-process
  Lean time is not a usable metric here: its range on the same file (0.65 to 1.71) is noise
  from start-up (the three runs of one mode differ by up to a factor of 2.5, e.g. dom08 S2 9.51,
  3.86, 4.80 s), while the check times repeat within 5 percent on most runs. The check time is the metric
  that carries the effect.
- Read as a design choice: embedding saves about 10 percent of the check against streaming the
  same file, and costs a factor of about 1.9 against the streamed default, because the keep
  mask and the binary format need the streamed route (`e2e.sbatch` stops on MODE=embed with
  MASK=1 or BIN=1). Embedding keeps one native axiom instead of two (checked with `#print axioms` on dom08: E
  `domino_8x8._native.native_decide.ax_1`; S1 and S2 add `domino_8x8.fwd._native…`).
- The streamed default's advantage in the check comes from part B (S2 part B is 1.8 to 3.1
  times faster than S1's on the same certificate); parts A and C are the same in S1 and S2.
